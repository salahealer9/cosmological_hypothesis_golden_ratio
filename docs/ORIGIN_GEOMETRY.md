# Geometric origin of the clue

The motivating right triangle uses

\[
a=1,\qquad b=\Phi^{-1}.
\]

Its squared hypotenuse is

\[
c^2=1+\Phi^{-2}=3-\Phi
=1.381966011250105\ldots,
\]

and its hypotenuse is

\[
c=\sqrt{3-\Phi}=1.175570504584946\ldots.
\]

This exact geometry is mathematically valid. The source diagram's additional formula \(a=\Phi/\sqrt c\) is not consistent with those definitions and is not used anywhere in the cosmological project.

The visual resemblance between \(1.381966\times 10\,\mathrm{Gyr}\) and the cosmic age was only a clue. The research hypothesis removes the fitted human unit and instead tests the dimensionless quantity

\[
H_0t_0\sqrt{\Omega_{\Lambda,0}}.
\]
