# Precise hypothesis and scope

## 1. Primary dimensionless claim

Let

\[
\Phi=\frac{1+\sqrt5}{2},\qquad
X(\theta)=H_0t_0\sqrt{\Omega_{\mathrm{de},0}},
\]

where \(\theta\) denotes the full cosmological parameter vector and \(t_0\) is the age predicted by that model. The primary hypothesis is

\[
\mathcal H_\Phi:\quad \Delta_\Phi(\theta)
\equiv X(\theta)-\Phi^{-1/2}=0.
\]

The primary test is performed first in spatially flat \(\Lambda\)CDM, so \(\Omega_{\mathrm{de},0}=\Omega_{\Lambda,0}\). It is then stress-tested in extensions.

## 2. Idealized analytic consequence

For flat matter + \(\Lambda\), neglecting radiation,

\[
E(a)^2=\Omega_ma^{-3}+\Omega_\Lambda,
\qquad \Omega_m+\Omega_\Lambda=1,
\]

and

\[
H_0t_0=\int_0^1\frac{da}{aE(a)}
=\frac{2}{3\sqrt{\Omega_\Lambda}}
\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt{\frac{\Omega_\Lambda}{\Omega_m}}.
\]

Therefore

\[
X=\frac23\operatorname{arsinh}\sqrt{\frac{\Omega_\Lambda}{\Omega_m}}.
\]

Setting \(X=\Phi^{-1/2}\) yields

\[
\frac{\Omega_\Lambda}{\Omega_m}
=\sinh^2\left(\frac{3}{2\sqrt\Phi}\right)
=2.1672895495558775\ldots
\]

and under flatness

\[
\Omega_m=0.3157273701547816\ldots,
\qquad
\Omega_\Lambda=0.6842726298452184\ldots.
\]

## 3. What the hypothesis does not yet explain

The equation is a constraint, not a mechanism. It does not yet derive \(\Phi\) from an action, symmetry, quantum state, field potential, attractor, or initial condition. It also does not explain why the relation should hold at the present epoch rather than at another time.

Those are separate theoretical requirements. Observational success would motivate that search; it would not replace it.

## 4. Discovery status

The relation was formulated after knowledge of modern estimates of \(H_0\), \(t_0\), and \(\Omega_\Lambda\). Consequently:

- Planck 2018 is part of the discovery context.
- Agreement with Planck 2018 cannot be labelled independent confirmation.
- A confirmatory claim must use analysis choices frozen in advance and data not used to construct or tune the relation.

## 5. Model extensions

The general background calculation uses

\[
E(a)^2=\Omega_ra^{-4}+\Omega_ma^{-3}+\Omega_ka^{-2}
+\Omega_{\mathrm{de}}a^{-3(1+w_0+w_a)}e^{-3w_a(1-a)}.
\]

The same diagnostic \(X=H_0t_0\sqrt{\Omega_{\mathrm{de},0}}\) can be calculated, but its physical interpretation becomes model-dependent. The extension tests are therefore robustness tests, not silent redefinitions of the primary hypothesis.
